# Hello World: the homepage as a DS-style RPG world

Date: 2026-11-10
Status: live on chiemjanssen.nl since 2026-11-10 (shipped straight to the homepage at Chiem's request, without the preview step).
Prototype: `_docs/superpowers/prototypes/hello-world/prototype.html` (self-contained, open in a browser). `hero.js` next to it is the character renderer it inlines. Like `_src/`, the prototype is local only and kept out of git via `.git/info/exclude`: scripts and tooling stay out of the repo.

The docs live under `_docs/` on purpose: GitHub Pages runs Jekyll, which skips folders starting with `_`, so specs and prototypes are never published.

## Intent

Chiem's current homepage (Instrument Serif, a three.js "field" background, quiet cards) reads as too hipster. He wants visitors to think "WOW, what a cool and unique portfolio." The new homepage is a small playable world in the style of a Nintendo DS era anime RPG: a pixel version of Chiem walks around an island where each company and project is a place you can visit.

It must still work as a portfolio. Anyone (client, partner, investor, curious developer) must be able to find what Chiem does and how to reach him within seconds, without playing.

Chiem is a founder, not a job seeker. Nothing on the site should read as "hire me". Contact means working together: a problem worth solving, a product to build, consultancy, technical sparring.

## Goals

- The homepage at `/`, `/nl/` and `/it/` becomes the full-viewport game world (after a preview period, see Go-live). No framed box: the canvas fills the browser window and the UI floats over it.
- The look and behaviour match the prototype, which Chiem has signed off on piece by piece (see "What's decided").
- Every venture, project and the contact route is reachable three ways: by walking, through the minimap (click to auto-walk), and through the menu (M), which lists everything with direct links.
- Works on desktop (keyboard and mouse) and phones (tap to walk).
- English, Dutch and Italian, as the site has today.
- Search engines and people who don't want to play get a plain page with the same content.

## Non-goals

- The Fitness (`/fitness/`) and Reef (`/reef/`) pages keep their current design. The game links to them.
- No sound or music in this version.
- No saved progress across visits (the visited counter resets per visit). Remembering the chosen language is fine.
- No analytics.

## Anti-goals (failure even if it technically works)

- It looks hipster or minimal again, or like a generic pixel template.
- A visitor cannot find what Chiem does, or how to contact him, without walking around.
- Any "hire me" or job-seeker wording.
- Copied art, characters, logos or music from Dragon Ball or any other existing game. "In the style of" only; all art is original.
- Scroll-jacking, or a page that traps keyboard or screen reader users.
- Slow or janky: it should feel instant on a mid-range phone.

## Constraints

- Static site on GitHub Pages (Jekyll, `CNAME` = chiemjanssen.nl). No server code.
- Pages are generated by `_src/pages.py` (`python3 _src/pages.py`) from one template per page, in en, nl and it. Copy is edited there, never in generated HTML. Language URLs: `/`, `/nl/`, `/it/`, with `hreflang` alternates and `x-default`.
- Copy rule from `pages.py`: plain sentences, no em or en dashes.
- Nothing is installed in this repo. `_src/build.sh` borrows rolldown and three.js from the Reef project. The game must not need three.js; prefer plain ES modules or a single script that needs no build step.
- Fonts from Google Fonts, as today: `Press Start 2P` (labels, logo, buttons) and `DotGothic16` (dialog and body text).

## What's decided (Chiem's calls, made while reviewing the prototype)

**Name.** The world is called **Hello World**. The minimap label and the welcome sign use the name. The page `<title>` and share title are **"Chiem Janssen · Hello World"**, so searches for his name still find him.

**Title screen.** "HELLO" in white over "WORLD" in gold, subtitle "The portfolio of Chiem Janssen", then "Click or press any key". So that nobody has to play to learn what Chiem does or how to reach him, the title screen also shows his role ("Software engineer & founder") and two small links: **Work together** (opens the email) and **Skip the game** (shows the plain page). Clicking them must not also start the game. The game stays the focus of the screen.

**Style.** DS-era anime RPG, top-down 3/4 view: lush shaded tiles, depth-sorted trees and buildings, a cliff with stairs and a waterfall, soft rounded edges between grass, paths, sand and water. Original art in that style, not 8-bit.

**Character.** Based on Chiem's GitHub photo: grey dad cap with a white script logo, dark brown hair, goatee and stubble, big squinty smile, navy knit zip-up, orange crossbody strap with a small black sling bag, dark baggy trousers, white low-top sneakers. Chiem is 181 cm; proportions are realistic rather than chibi. Four walking directions with a walk cycle. A larger portrait of the head and shoulders appears in dialog boxes, the name plate and the menu. In the prototype all of this is drawn in code from shaded primitives (`hero.js`); that approach worked and should be kept unless the builder finds a better route to the same look.

**The world (an island).**
- Plaza in the middle with a round cobbled square and a fountain. Spawn point is just south of it. A welcome sign explains the controls.
- **Localeo** (founder, since 2026): modern white and lavender glass office on the plateau above the cliff, reached by stone stairs. Speech bubble "Aa" sign.
- **Debugged** (co-founder, since 2019): brick office with a green neon `</>` sign.
- **D-care** (investor / technical lead, since 2024): red barn with silo and a fenced pig pen. **Pigs wear yellow ear tags.** **Gateways** (white box, two antennas, blinking status light, signal waves) sit on the barn roof and on a mast at the pen. Every few seconds an ear tag pings and a data packet arcs to a gateway. No "sensor poles": Chiem corrected that.
- **Fitness**: gym with a blue roof and a dumbbell sign.
- **Reef**: teal beach hut and a wooden pier into the sea. Interacting at the end of the pier gives the Reef pitch.
- **Whale shark**: a visible whale shark, not a shadow. It surfaces now and then somewhere in the sea near the player, swims for a while and dives again. Clicking it while it is up shows a "Rare sighting: whale shark" toast and a dialog that links to Reef. It should be a fun thing to spot, not something announced.
- Decoration: canal with wooden bridges, tulip fields, a windmill (joke dialog), crop field, trees, bushes, street lamps, rocks, a mailbox for contact.

**Interaction.**
- Title screen first; any key or click starts. The camera then follows the player and Chiem greets the visitor in a dialog.
- Move with WASD or the arrow keys, or click/tap a tile to walk there (pathfinding). Clicking a building walks to its door.
- Walking into a door, the mailbox, the sign, the fountain or the windmill opens a dialog. E, Enter or Space talks to what you face and confirms.
- Dialogs: portrait on the left, typewriter text written in Chiem's own voice, then a primary button (for example "Visit localeo.app", "Let's work together") and a dismiss button.
- HUD: name plate top left (portrait, "Chiem Janssen", "LV 1337", role), minimap top right (click to travel, shows the camera rectangle and the player), visited counter "n/6" (five places plus the mailbox) with a toast when all are visited, buttons for Menu and EN / NL / IT. Control hints bottom left on desktop.

**Menu (M).** Character sheet on the left: portrait, name, role, Level 1337, class, height ("181 cm, just under the Dutch average"), languages, "3 companies since 2019", and equipment (head: grey dad cap; body: navy knit zip-up; accessory: orange sling bag; feet: white low-tops). Tabs on the right:
- **Skills**: every skill maxed out, each with a full golden bar, "MAX" and a joke. Header "LV 1337 · XP: OVER 9000 · Next level: N/A". The skills and jokes are final as in the prototype (Full-stack engineering, Debugging, Founding companies, AI & sensors, Mobile apps, Interactive art, Shipping on Fridays, Languages, Height, Cap game).
- **Quests**: Localeo, D-care, Debugged, Fitness, Reef with role and year, a visited mark, and a Visit link.
- **Contact**: "Work together: got a problem worth solving? I help with new products, consultancy and technical sparring." with email, plus LinkedIn ("The serious version").
- The menu must fit the window at any size. The tabs stay put and only the list scrolls inside its panel. Chiem specifically checked this.

**Copy.** All in-game copy (dialogs, skills, jokes, menu, toasts, hints) exists in en, nl and it in the prototype's `I18N` object and is approved as a starting point. The nl and it texts are Claude's translations; Chiem will proofread them.

## Calls Claude made (Chiem can overrule)

- **Language switching uses the real URLs.** The EN / NL / IT buttons go to `/`, `/nl/`, `/it/`, so each language stays a real, indexable page. The prototype switches in place; the built site should navigate (keeping the player's position is a nice touch, not a requirement).
**Plain page (Chiem's call).** Each generated homepage contains the full content as semantic HTML: h1, role, every venture and project with its full description (as on today's homepage), logo, role, year and link, plus the Work together contact and LinkedIn. It is what search engines index, what shows without JavaScript, and what "Skip the game" reveals (on the title screen, in the menu, and as the first keyboard stop on the page). It is **styled in the same DS pixel look as the menu panels** (same fonts, rounded white and blue panels, gold buttons, the portrait), not today's card design and not bare HTML. It has a "Play Hello World" button back to the game.

**Go-live (Chiem's call).** Build and publish it first on a hidden, unlinked path (for example `/hello-world/`, `/hello-world/nl/`, `/hello-world/it/`, with `noindex`) so Chiem can test it on his phone and proofread the Dutch and Italian. Only after his sign-off does it replace the homepage at `/`, `/nl/`, `/it/` and the preview path is removed.
- **Reduced motion.** With `prefers-reduced-motion`, keep the world but stop the decorative animation (water shimmer, windmill, packets, whale shark), and make camera moves instant.
- **Open Graph images.** Replace `assets/og-*.jpg` with a screenshot of the title screen per language.
- **Old homepage assets.** The homepage stops loading `assets/field.js`, but the Reef pages still use it, so it stays. The homepage parts of `site.css` can go once the swap is done, if nothing else uses them.
- **One source for copy.** The in-game copy moves into `_src/pages.py` next to the rest of the site copy, so every language is edited in one place. In-game links to Fitness and Reef point to the matching language (`/nl/fitness/` and so on).
- **After a language switch** the title screen is skipped, so switching doesn't feel like starting over.
- **Accents.** Fix accented characters the pixel font lacks (for example "ABILITÀ"), with a fallback font or another approach.
- **Mobile.** Zoom level 2 on phones. The minimap is hidden, and Menu and the language buttons sit in the bottom corner. Tap to walk is the main control; a D-pad is optional.

## Left to the builder

- Code structure, file layout, and whether the game ships as one script or a few ES modules.
- Whether sprites are generated at runtime (as in the prototype) or pre-rendered at build time, as long as the look holds.
- Exact map layout, as long as the places, the cliff with Localeo on top, the canal, the beach with the pier, and the overall feel stay the same.
- Performance work (only draw what is on screen, pause when the tab is hidden, handle device pixel ratio cleanly).
- Accessibility details beyond the above: focus handling for dialogs and the menu, `aria-live` for dialog text, Escape to close.

## Known gaps in the prototype

- Tested in headless Chrome at desktop sizes and checked at 1024×640 to 1440×900. The phone layout was only lightly checked; headless Chrome would not render a true 390 px viewport.
- Dialog CSS animations and the title fade did not complete under headless virtual time; they look right in a normal browser but should be verified.
- The pixel font drops accents (the "À" in "ABILITÀ" shows as "A"); see Accents above.
- Prototype-only test hooks in the URL hash (`#at…`, `#shark`, `#menu`, `#lang=`, `#name=`) should not ship.
