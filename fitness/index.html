Everything that gets you there

Whether you want to lose fat, keep your weight or build muscle, the app works out a daily calorie and protein target for you and keeps adjusting it as your weight changes.

Train with a plan

  • Ready-made programs: full body, push / pull / legs or upper / lower, at the gym, with dumbbells or at home
  • Log sets fast: last time's weights and reps are already filled in
  • Photos and instructions for almost 200 exercises
  • Personal records, gym streaks and your full history
  • Train together with a friend through a QR code (or NFC on Android)

Eat for your goal

  • A daily calorie and macro target based on your body, goal and training days
  • The target adapts to your real progress, not just a formula
  • Built-in food table, barcode scanning and millions of products
  • Plan meals ahead and add your usual meals in one tap

Track your progress

  • A weight trend that smooths out the daily ups and downs
  • Your goal date: on plan and at your current pace
  • Body measurements and private progress photos
  • Friendly reminders on training days

A coach that stays on your phone

  • Ask how you are doing or what to eat tonight
  • It can suggest a meal, log your weight or open your workout, always after you confirm
  • Runs completely on your phone: your chats never leave it

Private by design

  • No account, no sign-up
  • Everything is stored encrypted on your phone
  • Lock the app with a PIN, Face ID or fingerprint
  • Export all your data and take it to a new phone

Free, with at most one ad a day

  • All features are free
  • A one-time purchase removes the ads for good
  • If the app helps you, you can leave a tip
  • In English, Dutch, Italian, German, French and Spanish

Set your goal in a minute and get a plan for training and eating that fits it.
