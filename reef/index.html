Reef

An endless reef, grown by everyone who visits.

0 m · The surface

Beauty first, then the message.

Reef is one long reef, seen from the side, projected large in a dark room. Left to right it never ends. Top to bottom it goes from the sunlit coral reef to the twilight reef and down to the dark cold-water reef, where a lamp is the only light.

On the first day it is mostly bare seabed. Every visitor adds something, so by the end of a show the reef is full of life that people made together.

30 m · How it works

Three questions. One creature. Yours.

  1. Answer a few questions

    At the kiosk, a few playful questions about how you see yourself. No camera, no account.

  2. A real species joins the reef

    A local AI picks a real reef animal or coral from a verified species library, shaped by your answers, and it swims in at its real depth.

  3. Name it, take it home

    You give it a name and get a card with a QR code that brings you back to it, any time.

80 m · What we do to the water

Part of the reef is polluted.

The cause always fits the place, and every one comes from the real world. Explore the reef and you see the contrast between the vivid, protected parts and the parts we damaged.

  • Garbage thrown overboardFrom a boat, sinking onto the shallow reef.
  • Outfall pipesSewage and chemicals flowing straight into the sea.
  • Mine wasteTailings sliding down the slope and smothering coral.
  • Bottom trawlingA trawler far above, its net scraping the cold-water reef.

150 m · A living world

Day and night. Calm and storm.

The reef lives on its own time. Night brings out the long-spined urchins, storms stir up the water, and now and then something big passes by.
